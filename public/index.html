How it works

From idea to live automation in three steps

01

Design your flow

Build dialog scenarios, branching logic, and automation rules in the visual constructor — no developers needed.

02

Connect your channels

Plug in any messenger or communication channel. Manage every conversation from a unified workspace.

03

Launch and optimise

Deploy your bot, enable AI answers from your knowledge base, and track performance as it runs.

Platform

Built for modern communication

Everything you need to automate, engage, and scale.

Visual flow builder

Dialog scenarios with branching, conditions, loops, and subflows. Complex automation, built visually.

Multichannel engine

Connect any messenger or channel. One platform, one contact base, one inbox.

AI knowledge base

Upload documents and let retrieval answer questions automatically, with confidence scoring.

Smart broadcasting

Segment the audience with flexible filters, schedule campaigns, and track delivery in real time.

Multi-bot architecture

Run unlimited assistants for different departments or audiences, each with its own flows.

Modular extensions

Extend with Solutions and Plugins built against public contracts — never against Core internals.

Ideas

Same blocks, pointed at different problems

None of these is a separate product. Each one is a flow, a channel, and a contact list — assembled from the blocks above. Here is what teams put together first.

HR & people ops

Onboarding sequences, 360° reviews, pulse surveys, and an FAQ bot for company policy.

Retail & e-commerce

Order status updates, product catalogues, loyalty programmes, and assisted support across channels.

Education

Student onboarding, assignment reminders, a Q&A bot, and automated exam notifications.

Healthcare

Appointment reminders, symptom questionnaires, patient FAQ, and post-visit feedback.

Real estate

Property catalogues, viewing scheduling, lead qualification, and follow-up sequences.

Finance & banking

Client onboarding, document collection, an FAQ bot, and transaction alerts.

Logistics & delivery

Shipment tracking, driver communication, warehouse notifications, and delivery confirmations.

Hospitality

Check-in flows, a concierge bot, guest feedback collection, and upsell campaigns.

Legal services

Document requests, deadline reminders, client communication, and case status updates.

IT & SaaS

Onboarding sequences, support ticket routing, incident notifications, and feedback loops.

NGO & government

Citizen communication, event registration, community feedback, and public service automation.

Franchises & chains

Staff training across locations, compliance checks, and multi-location broadcasts.
